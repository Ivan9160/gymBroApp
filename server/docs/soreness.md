# Mathematical Specification of the Soreness Service

## Overview
This document outlines the mathematical formulas and physiological models used to calculate a user's muscle group soreness (DOMS). The system evaluates recent training data, factors in the proximity to failure (RIR), exercise-specific relative intensity, mechanical volume, and the user's overall proficiency to generate an adaptive fatigue score. The model relies on a linear time decay function to simulate recovery.

The underlying business logic is implemented within `SorenessService` and draws global configuration variables from `SorenessConfig`.

---

## 1. Global Parameters (`soreness.config.ts`)

The calculations rely on the following constants defined in the configuration layer:

* **`SORENESS_LIMIT`** ($L_{\text{base}}$): **200.0** — The baseline stress threshold that represents 100% muscle soreness for a standard user.
* **`RECOVERY_DAYS`** ($T_{\text{recovery}}$): **7** days — The total duration over which accumulated stress linearly decays to zero.

---

## 2. Core Calculations & Formulas

### 2.1. Individual Set Stress ($S_{\text{set}}$)
**Invoked by function:** `calculateStressForSet(set: ISet, proficiency: number, bodyweight: number, now: number)`

This function evaluates the systemic fatigue generated by an individual set, scaling it against the user's proven physical capacity and exponential failure proximity.

#### Step A: Effective Weight & Safety Constraints ($m_{\text{safe}}$)
The system calculates the effective mass lifted, factoring in bodyweight for calisthenic movements:

$$m_{\text{eff}} = \begin{cases} m_{\text{user}} + m_{\text{added}}, & \text{if } \texttt{isBodyweight} = \text{true} \\ m_{\text{added}}, & \text{if } \texttt{isBodyweight} = \text{false} \end{cases}$$

To prevent mathematical anomalies where recorded weights exceed the theoretical 1RM, the system calculates an Epley estimate for the current set and clamps the effective weight:

$$\text{1RM}_{\text{est}} = m_{\text{eff}} \cdot \left(1 + \frac{R}{30}\right)$$
$$m_{\text{safe}} = \min(m_{\text{eff}}, \text{1RM}_{\text{est}} \cdot 0.99)$$

* $R$ = Number of repetitions completed (`set.reps`).

#### Step B: User Theoretical Capacity ($C_{\text{max}}$)
The user's true capacity for the exercise is derived from the exercise benchmark ($B$) and their muscle group proficiency ($P$):

$$D = B + \begin{cases} m_{\text{user}}, & \text{if } \texttt{isBodyweight} = \text{true} \\ 0, & \text{if } \texttt{isBodyweight} = \text{false} \end{cases}$$
$$C_{\text{max}} = P \cdot D$$

#### Step C: Pure Intensity & Reps in Reserve (RIR)
Pure intensity ($I_{\text{pure}}$) represents how close the safe working weight is to the user's true 1RM, bounded strictly between **0.1** (10%) and **1.0** (100%) to ensure mathematical stability:

$$I_{\text{pure}} = \max\left(0.1, \min\left(1.0, \frac{m_{\text{safe}}}{C_{\text{max}}}\right)\right)$$

The Reps in Reserve (RIR) are then calculated using a reverse Epley formula:

$$R_{\text{max}} = 30 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{I_{\text{pure}}} - 1\right)$$
$$\text{RIR} = \max(0, R_{\text{max}} - R)$$

#### Step D: Relative Intensity & Base Stress
Intensity is scaled by the exercise's structural importance ($K_{\text{exercise}}$). Sets with a relative intensity below **0.3** (30%) are discarded as "junk volume" (Stress = 0).

$$I_{\text{rel}} = I_{\text{pure}} \cdot K_{\text{exercise}}$$

Fatigue scales exponentially as the user approaches failure, governed by $F_{\text{prox}}$, and quadratically with intensity:

$$F_{\text{prox}} = e^{-0.4 \cdot \text{RIR}}$$
$$S_{\text{base}} = R \cdot (I_{\text{rel}})^2 \cdot F_{\text{prox}} \cdot 10$$

#### Step E: Linear Time Decay
Stress degrades linearly over the predefined recovery window:

$$t_{\text{hours}} = \frac{t_{\text{now}} - t_{\text{set}}}{1000 \cdot 3600}$$
$$F_{\text{time}} = \max\left(0, 1 - \frac{t_{\text{hours}}}{T_{\text{recovery}} \cdot 24}\right)$$
$$S_{\text{set}} = S_{\text{base}} \cdot F_{\text{time}}$$

---

### 2.2. Muscle Group Aggregation
**Invoked by function:** `calculateSorenessForMuscleGroup(sets: ISet[], proficiency: number, profile: IUserProfile)`

The system sums the decayed stress of all sets within a specific muscle group. To account for the **Repeated Bout Effect**, the overall soreness limit is scaled dynamically based on the user's proficiency—highly trained users require more stress to achieve 100% soreness.

$$L_{\text{adapt}} = L_{\text{base}} \cdot P$$
$$\text{Soreness (\%)} = \min\left(100, \frac{\sum S_{\text{set}}}{L_{\text{adapt}}} \cdot 100\right)$$
